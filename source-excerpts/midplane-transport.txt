The origin is geometric: at the mid-plane $\overline{v}_y=0$ by symmetry, blocking 1-e-Fluid transport into the sheet, while the sub-population velocities $\overline{v}_{1y}$ and $\overline{v}_{2y}$ -- present in 2-e-Fluid and PIC but absent by construction in 1-e-Fluid -- carry plasma across from opposite sides supplying density to the layer.
